What is implemented

ITU-T T.4 one-dimensional Modified Huffman coding: white/black run-length terminating and makeup codes, per-line EOL, bit packing, and RTC on export. Input is normalized to 1728 pixels, monochrome. The .vfax container stores a small header (dimensions and resolution) plus raw T.4 MH data; it is a VOICEFAX wrapper, not a standardized interchange container. Raw .g3 streams can be decoded when dimensions are supplied.

Standards boundary & dependencies

No third-party libraries or backend are required for PNG/JPEG, MH, and WAV generation. PDF import uses Mozilla PDF.js loaded from jsDelivr; it is optional and processes pages locally. T.4 two-dimensional MR, TIFF-G3 packaging, T.30 signaling, V.17/V.29/V.27ter modem data modulation, and audio demodulation/received-page reconstruction are not implemented. The WAV feature only generates the standard CNG calling tone, not fax page data.
