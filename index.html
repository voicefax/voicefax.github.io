How to test

  1. Open this page on two devices using HTTPS (GitHub Pages is fine).
  2. On the receiving device, tap Start listening and allow microphone access.
  3. On the sending device, choose a small PDF or image, tap Prepare file, then Send by sound.
  4. Keep the devices close, with the sender speaker aimed toward the receiver microphone.
  5. When playback finishes, tap Stop & decode on the receiving device.

The first build uses a simple audible multitone packet modem and a recording-based decoder. Browser audio processing, automatic gain control, echo cancellation, and room noise can affect results. It is a prototype, not a guaranteed high-speed fax standard.
