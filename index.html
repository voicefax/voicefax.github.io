How it works

Make the connection

Press Listen on the receiving browser, then Send over sound on the other browser or device. Keep the devices physically near each other with volume turned up.

Sound protocol: files are split into chunks, encoded in low-frequency audio tones, and reconstructed by the receiver. Use short files for reliable transfer. Real fax modulation and printer equipment are not represented as compatible T.30/T.4 fax endpoints.
