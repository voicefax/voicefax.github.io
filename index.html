How to use VoiceFax

  1. Open VoiceFax on two devices.
  2. On the receiving device, press Start listening.
  3. On the sending device, choose a photograph.
  4. Press Transmit photo.
  5. Keep the devices close together while the audio plays.
  6. On the receiving device, press Stop and decode after transmission finishes.
