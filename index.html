How to use VoiceFax

  1. Open VoiceFax on two devices.
  2. On the receiving device, press Start listening.
  3. On the sending device, write a message or choose a photo or file.
  4. Press Transmit message.
  5. Keep the devices close together while the modem sounds play.
  6. The receiving device will detect, decode, and save the transmission.
